# Code of Conduct

CohortHarbor contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, specific and focused on improving the project.

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Give review feedback with evidence and a concrete path forward.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, workplace data or confidential documents.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or deliberately disrupt other participants.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when behavior threatens people, project safety or confidentiality. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the GitHub repository; do not expose sensitive details in a public issue.
